TITLE:
The last person to catch smallpox naturally 💉

CAPTION:
On 26 October 1977, Ali Maow Maalin in Merca, Somalia became the last natural smallpox case. Exactly 2 years later, WHO experts certified smallpox eradicated, the only human disease ever wiped out. 💉
