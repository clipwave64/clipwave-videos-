The day we learned other galaxies exist 🌌

CAPTION:
On Nov 23, 1924, the New York Times reported that Edwin Hubble had found Andromeda is a separate galaxy, thanks to a Cepheid star he spotted with the 100-inch Hooker telescope. Before that, many thought the Milky Way was the whole universe.
