TITLE:
The only animal that poops cubes 🧊

CAPTION:
Wombats make cube-shaped droppings, and studies by Georgia Tech and the University of Tasmania found the shape forms in the intestine, thanks to stiff and flexible sections of its wall. They leave the cubes on rocks and logs to communicate. 🐾
