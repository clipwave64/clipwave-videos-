TITLE:
Why X-rays are called X-rays ❓

CAPTION:
Wilhelm Röntgen discovered X-rays on Nov 8, 1895, and called them 'X' because he didn't know what they were. He won the first Nobel Prize in Physics in 1901 and refused to patent it. 🦴
