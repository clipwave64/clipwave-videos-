TITLE:
Why the clocks go back this weekend ⏰

CAPTION:
Clocks in Europe go back 1 hour on Sunday, October 25 (US: November 1). Germany was the first country to adopt daylight saving time nationwide in 1916, to save fuel in WWI. ⏰
