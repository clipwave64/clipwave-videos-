TITLE:
Octopuses have 3 hearts and blue blood 🐙

CAPTION:
Two hearts pump blood through the gills, and the third pumps it to the body, but it stops when the octopus swims! Their blood is blue because it uses copper-based hemocyanin instead of iron.
