TITLE:
The first internet message crashed after 2 letters 💻

CAPTION:
On Oct 29, 1969, UCLA tried to send "LOGIN" over ARPANET to the Stanford Research Institute. The system crashed after "LO", so the first message ever was just two letters. 💻
