TITLE:
This animal has a million hairs per square inch 🦦

CAPTION:
Sea otters have 500,000 to 1,000,000 hairs per square inch, the densest fur of any animal. With no blubber, that fur (and eating ~25% of their body weight a day) keeps them warm. 🦦
