TITLE:
Hubble launched blurry. Astronauts gave it glasses 👓

CAPTION:
Hubble's 2.4 m mirror was ground about 2 microns too flat at the edge, so its first images were fuzzy. In December 1993 astronauts installed corrective optics, and it has made over 1.5 million observations since.
