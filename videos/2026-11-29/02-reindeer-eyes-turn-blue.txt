TITLE:
Reindeer eyes turn blue in winter 🦌

CAPTION:
A 2013 study found the mirror layer behind reindeer retinas shifts from gold in summer to blue in winter. Researchers say it makes their eyes far more sensitive to light during the long Arctic darkness.
