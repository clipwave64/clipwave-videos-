Ford cut car building from 12 hours to 93 minutes 🚗

CAPTION:
In 1913 Ford's Highland Park plant put the Model T chassis on a moving assembly line, inspired by Chicago meatpackers. Build time fell from over 12 hours to 93 minutes, and more than 15 million Model Ts were made.
