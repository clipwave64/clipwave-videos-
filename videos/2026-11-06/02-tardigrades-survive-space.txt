This tiny animal survived the vacuum of space 🚀

CAPTION:
In 2007, dried-out tardigrades rode the FOTON-M3 satellite and were exposed to open space for 10 days. Many revived after rehydration and some still reproduced (Jönsson et al., Current Biology 2008). 🐻
