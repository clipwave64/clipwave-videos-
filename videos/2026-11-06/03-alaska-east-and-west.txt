Alaska is the most EASTERN state too 🧭

CAPTION:
Alaska's Aleutian Islands cross the 180th meridian, so islands like Semisopochnoi lie in the Eastern Hemisphere. That makes Alaska the northernmost, westernmost and (by longitude) easternmost US state. 🗺️
