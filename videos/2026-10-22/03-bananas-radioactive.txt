TITLE:
Bananas are radioactive (and so are you) 🍌

CAPTION:
Bananas contain potassium, and a tiny fraction of potassium is radioactive potassium-40. One banana is about 0.1 microsieverts, roughly 1% of a normal day's natural background radiation. Totally safe!
