TITLE:
The first close-up of Mars was colored by hand 🖍️

CAPTION:
Mariner 4 launched on Nov 28, 1964 and sent back the first close-up photos of Mars at about 8 bits per second. JPL engineers couldn't wait, so they colored the first image by hand from printed numbers.
