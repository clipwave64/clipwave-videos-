# Your own gameplay recordings

Put screen recordings that **you made yourself** here (e.g. you playing Subway Surfers on your phone).
The daily run will use a random muted piece of one of them on the bottom half of each video.

How to record and upload:
1. On your phone, turn on Do Not Disturb (so no notifications show up), then start the screen recorder
   (iPhone: Control Center > Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings > Screen record).
2. Play normally for 2-5 minutes, then stop recording. A few different recordings is even better.
3. On github.com, open this `gameplay` folder > **Add file** > **Upload files**, drag the video(s) in,
   and click **Commit changes**. Keep each file under 25 MB (shorter or lower-quality recordings).

Do NOT put clips downloaded from YouTube/TikTok here: they belong to whoever recorded them.
